The Ruined City

Decades after the last bus left, the city has become a wild place. Deer graze the old fields, foxes hunt after dark, crows wheel over broken towers, and the weather keeps its own council — sun, fog, rain, and night, all day. West of town the cathedral stands alone in the west meadow, its broken flagging overgrown, one spire still reaching, its nave slowly giving way. A river winds through the eastern hills and opens into a sea cove at the map's edge, where old boats still lie wrecked. Behind the sea cliffs, the hills grow dark with an ancient evergreen forest, and in a clearing stands a stone circle far older than the city. In the open water beyond the cove, at night, something enormous surfaces now and then. Everything you see is real and walkable: there is no backdrop wall, only a city lost in the hills.
