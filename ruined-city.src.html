The Ruined City

Decades after the last bus left, the city has become a wild place. Deer graze the old fields, foxes hunt after dark, crows wheel over broken towers, and the weather keeps its own council — sun, fog, rain, and night, all day. West of town the cathedral stands alone on its stone podium, one spire still reaching, its nave slowly giving way. A river winds through the eastern hills and opens into a lake at the map's edge, where old boats still lie wrecked. Everything you see is real and walkable: there is no backdrop wall, only a city lost in the hills.
